Privacy Policy

Effective 22 September 2026 · PromptFly for Android and iPhone

PromptFly is a teleprompter, camera, script writer and video editor for your phone. This page explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short answer: it stays on your phone.

The short version

  • What you make stays on your phone. PromptFly has no accounts and no server, and we never receive your scripts, recordings or videos.
  • The free version shows ads from Google, and Google receives information about your device to show them. Pro removes the ads, and the app then asks for none.
  • AI script writing is optional. When you use it, what you submit goes straight from your phone to the AI service you picked, using your own key.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting, nothing sold.

Who we are

PromptFly is made by Mohammad Mahbub (“we”). If you have a question about this policy or your information, email abmahbub08@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Everything you make and set up in PromptFly is kept in the app’s private storage on your phone:

  • your scripts, recordings, edited projects and exported videos;
  • your settings, such as teleprompter and camera preferences;
  • any AI service key you add, which is kept in your phone’s secure storage (the Android Keystore or the iPhone Keychain);
  • whether you own Pro and, in the free version, when your phone was last online.

We have no way to see any of this. It is removed when you delete it in the app, or when you uninstall PromptFly. Exported videos are also saved to your phone’s gallery, where they stay until you delete them there.

What PromptFly asks permission for

  • Camera — to record video.
  • Microphone — to record sound, and for dictation when you use it.
  • Photos or storage — to add exported videos to your gallery. Android 11 and later need no permission for this. On iPhone, PromptFly asks only to add to your photos, never to read them.
  • Face ID, Touch ID or fingerprint — only if you turn on App Lock. Your phone does the check; PromptFly is told only whether it succeeded.
  • Internet — for ads, AI script writing and the free version’s connection check.

PromptFly does not ask for your location, contacts or Bluetooth. A Bluetooth remote works because your phone treats it as a keyboard.

Ads in the free version

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the home screen and in the script editor, and now and then a full-screen ad after you finish a recording or an export. Google’s ad software receives the information in the table above to show ads, limit how often you see the same one, measure them and prevent fraud. PromptFly asks Google for non-personalised ads, and never shares your scripts, recordings or videos with Google.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, Google’s consent form asks you before any ad is shown, and you can change your answer at any time in Settings › Ad privacy choices. On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings. Google explains how it uses this information at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

With Pro, PromptFly shows no ads and requests none.

AI script writing

AI features stay off until you add a key from a service you choose: Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter, or an address you enter yourself. What you submit is sent directly from your phone to that service, never through us, and the service handles it under its own terms and privacy policy. Any cost is billed to your account with them. Your key never leaves your phone except to that service, is never included in backups, and you can remove it in PromptFly’s AI settings.

Dictation

Dictation uses the speech recognition built into your phone — usually Google’s or Samsung’s on Android, and Apple’s on iPhone. Depending on your phone and its settings, that service may process your voice on its own servers, under its provider’s privacy policy. PromptFly receives only the resulting text.

Buying Pro

Pro is sold through Google Play or the App Store, which handle the payment under their own policies. We never see your card or payment details. PromptFly is told only that you own Pro, and remembers that on your phone.

The connection check

The free version needs an internet connection at least once a day to start new recordings, edits and exports. To check, it sends an empty request to connectivitycheck.gstatic.com, an address Google runs for exactly this purpose. Nothing about you or what you make is included. PromptFly remembers only when the check last succeeded. Pro skips the check entirely.

Backups

A PromptFly backup file contains your scripts and settings. It does not contain recordings, videos or AI keys. You choose where the file is saved or who it is sent to.

What PromptFly does not do

  • It has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • It has no analytics and no crash reporting.
  • It does not sell, rent or trade information about you.
  • It does not collect your location.
  • It does not itself track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Children

PromptFly is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Ads in the free version are limited to Google’s “PG” content rating.

How long information is kept

We keep nothing, because nothing reaches us. What PromptFly stores on your phone stays there until you delete it or uninstall the app. Information received by Google, your AI service, your phone’s speech service or the app store is kept under their own policies.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete any script, recording or project in the app. Uninstalling PromptFly removes everything it stored, except videos you saved to your gallery.
  • Remove ads, and the ad requests with them, by buying Pro.
  • Change your ad consent in Settings › Ad privacy choices, where it is offered.
  • Remove your AI key at any time.
  • Turn off any permission in your phone’s settings.

Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing on our side to access, correct or delete. For information handled by Google, your AI service or your phone’s speech service, use their controls or contact them directly. If anything here is unclear, email us and we will answer.

If you are in Europe or the UK

Data protection law (the GDPR and the UK GDPR) asks for a reason behind each use of your information:

  • Ads — your consent, given in Google’s consent form. You can withdraw it at any time in Settings › Ad privacy choices.
  • AI script writing and dictation — providing a feature you asked for. Neither runs unless you use it.
  • Buying Pro — completing your purchase.
  • The connection check — our legitimate interest in running the free version as this policy describes. It carries nothing about you beyond the request itself.

Google, your AI service and your phone’s speech service may process information outside your country, under their own safeguards. You have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

PromptFly keeps your work in storage that other apps cannot read, keeps keys in your phone’s secure storage, and offers an optional App Lock. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your phone itself locked.

Changes to this policy

If PromptFly changes how it handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches you.
